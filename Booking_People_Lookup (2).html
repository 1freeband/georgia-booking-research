All 159 Georgia counties · Searches filtered by name, city, and age · Your progress is saved in this browser

Paste one or more booking lists. Use the format LAST, FIRST (age) — date — charges. County headings like — Richmond — or — Cobb County — set the county for the names below them. Names under no heading use the default county.

📞 One-time setup: grab phones and relatives with one click. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: 📞 Grab to Lookup

Flow (1 tap per person): paste the inmate → Build (TruePeopleSearch opens) → tap the right match → tap 📞 Grab. It saves that page and automatically jumps to the next relative/associate. Just keep tapping 📞 Grab on each page until it says ALL DONE, then switch back here. Phones, addresses, skipped people and your call queue all fill in on their own. On Android Chrome, run the bookmark by typing its name in the address bar and tapping it. If nothing fills in, tap that person's Paste button.

📲 Bulk call queue Builds a list of every wireless number from offenders, relatives and associates you've grabbed. Repeated numbers are only listed once.
